## They/she ⋮ Gnoll
The eldest. In her loom she weaves a complex and ever-changing pattern; some say it is a history, others a prophecy. Only the weaver knows for sure. They are also skilled with the *patterning frame*, and are always on the lookout for some new bead or stone to add to it, providing it fits the pattern.
