## What songs are you best known for? 
_Harper’s Devotion_. A sad, short tale about a musician that falls in love with a moon. Start with a **Celestial Lute**. Reveals the constellations above, no matter the weather.
## What pays your way across the land?
**Performance.** Performing at taverns always yields both room and board. Sometimes you even get tips! Start with an extra **10gp**.
## Bond
You inherited an old **Journal**, bound in bark. Each evening, its pages are filled with the events of the day, crassly written from the journal’s perspective. The writing is crude but accurate.

## Omen
Hunters talk of a curse that befalls any who kill a beast with a streak of white fur: soon after, they are found dead in their homes. Each day, there are fewer and fewer creatures to hunt.
## Growth

### Charming and Open
When you use charm or charisma to your advantage, roll **WIL**. On a hit, hold 2. On a miss, you and your target both hold 1. Spend these hold, 1-for-1, to ask the following questions. 
- How can I get them to [x]?
- What do they find so charming about me? 
- What are they not telling me? 
- What do they desire?
- What are they afraid will happen next? 

### Bond of Blood 
You are bound by blood and smoke to the homunculus known as [['Greg']]. The bond is fresh, and unstable. Your health pools are currently linked, meaning that damage taken is shared between you both. 

As the bond develops it may yet continue to change. 

## Unique Items
### Pebble of the Raging River
*3 uses*
When hurled at the ground, creates a 10ft cube of water at the site of impact. *Recharge*: Submerge in a river for a month. 

### Cool Cup
*3 uses*
An excellent vessel. Any liquid poured into this cup is purified of contaminants. *Recharge*: Craft a cup of similar quality, then drink from it.
