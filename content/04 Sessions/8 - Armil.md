## Summary
*(We invented Esty, which is "Etsy, but for rivers".)*

The crew of *the Damselfly* rests in Armil for a few days. Veda recovers from their concussion while the others go about town, resupply, research, wander, and make coin.

During their last night in the fungiwood city they share a big meal around the fire. Lu returns with a bottle of mead, Greg collects wood, Brim cooks. January puts on a show for the party, sharing a story from his childhood. A good time is had by all.

## Notable Moments
Certain that Brim is, or was, Spark—a young girl that traveled with the troupe, and was later abandoned by them—Cheò takes the two of them about the city in recreation of a day they shared together nearly a decade ago. They visit several locations, each unsuccessful in triggering her memories. 

It isn't until Cheò tells Brim the story of Spark, how the troupe left her behind, that Brim realizes that she *was* this other girl, whatever that means, and the two reconcile. Everyone cried.
