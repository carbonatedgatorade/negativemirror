## Summary
The party departs from [[Coratón|Coratón]] in Lu's new boat! "New" in that it's new to Lu. The boat also comes with Vern, Lu's younger cousin, who has traveled here all the way from [[Llyff-on-the-99]] to see the world with Lu and co. 

Traveling down W-47, they make their way to the city [[Armil]] to witness to iron harvest. 

## Notable Moments
- Everyone had a good time repairing the boat! Jan even got a good deal on tin.
- Veda tapped into the Howl for the first time and saw a vision of an enormous skull somewhere in the riverlands.
- Discovered the apiarists [[Xanthe and Oia]], and got some especially energizing honey from them! ***1 use*** *Next **DEX** roll has advantage.*
- Met [[Gunny]], the Mender, who placed a charm of guidance on the boat. *Once per session you can re-roll a boat-based wilderness event*.
- Discovered the **Deep Fissure**, a crack in the world that exhales warm air. 
- Brim spotted the crow! You know, the one from her dream? She and the party follow it and discover the cool skull Veda saw. Laying in front of the skull are two things:
	- **A white raven feather** (petty): A large feather of that color called *argent*, a white that contains an almost moon-like iridescence. ***1 use** You may fall from any height unharmed.*
	- **Seeing stone** (petty): A light, hand-held stone in the shape of an eye.  *Choose a created object. You can sense the nearest example*. **Recharge:** Leave in a bucket of rainwater overnight. 
- Cheò, Vern, and Greg do some planting and talking. 
- As the party nears Armil they encounter a hydra and have a big fight about it! It's awesome, and everyone kicks a bunch of ass. Veda gets a *reorienting headwound!* But is otherwise fine. (: 
- In the aftermath of the battle, which was witnessed by the Outrider [[Leonia]], the party takes a moment to tend to one another and to the boat. 
- They are now in Armil, and have the good word of the local Outrider, which is bound to garner a bit of favor.
