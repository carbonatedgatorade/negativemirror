### Faction Resolution 

#### The Senate of Wolves
Veda was visited in dream by the Senate of Wolves. They were told that their service has been noted, and their debt has been cleared. Veda and Dirtbike were both granted additional privileges among canines the world over, and the ability to freely reference *The Howl* and receive more direct information from it. 

#### The Chatelain Hisvet
The Chatelain is interested in sponsoring house Limnē, granting a small holding within the city and Lu the title of *optimate*. If he wanted, Lu could also designate a *gendarme*, or *man-at-arms*. Hisvet made the strong implication that she was talking about January. When questioned, she simply said that she thought Lu had a lot of promise, and was interested in investing early. 

The crew got together at the ship to do a group read on the situation. Cheò thought he should do it. January wasn't sure at first, but came around. Veda and Brim both wondered if Lu's goals would always align with Hisvet's, and if it was worth getting entangled.


> [!quote] Cheò and Lu
> <p align="left"><b>Cheò</b>: We fought a star and won.</p>
> <p align="right"><b>Lu</b>: That's a good point.</p>

# Wrapping Up 
The crew decides that they will travel to [[The Garden of Memory]], as they are interested in what secrets it might hold—of the world and of themselves.

Late one day, Cheò is approached by Donelek, a member of Hisvet's retinue. He tells Cheò that their *aurifex* back home is greatly relieved that he is safe, and asks Cheò to come back to the camp in the center of town. 

Inside the tent Cheò found a magic book, linked to another sitting on a desk hundreds of miles away, with his brother Tamhas on the other end. Writing messages back and forth to each other, the two reconciled and basically everyone at the table cried.
